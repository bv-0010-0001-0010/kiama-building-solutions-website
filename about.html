About Kiama

A building partner, not just a quote.

Kiama Building Solutions helps homeowners across Melbourne's east get renovations, extensions and new builds done properly — and helps other builders get reliable crews on site when they need them.

What Kiama does

Two needs, one point of contact.

Kiama Building Solutions Pty Ltd designs, manages and delivers residential building work for homeowners, and supplies construction labour and skilled trades to other builders and contractors. The business exists to do two related things properly: get your own project built right, and get the right people on site when someone else's project needs them.

Director Komalben Mehta oversees both sides of the business personally, so a renovation client and a site supervisor calling for a crew are dealing with the same person — not different departments, and not a relay through someone else.

Leadership

Led by Komalben Mehta

Komalben Mehta founded Kiama Building Solutions to bring a straightforward standard to residential building — clear written quotes, one point of contact from first call to handover, and a program that's actually kept to. That same standard carries over to the crews Kiama supplies to other builders: matched to the job, briefed before they arrive, and accountable to one person.

How we work

Built for how you live

Work is planned and staged around the household, not the other way around — keeping the home liveable wherever that's possible.

Direct communication

You deal with Kiama directly. No account layers, no relay, no waiting for someone to call someone else.

Reliability over volume

A smaller number of well-run projects and placements is worth more than a big number that turns over every month.

Safety & quality

Safety is set by the site and upheld by the crew.

Every project has its own safety requirements, induction and supervision structure. Kiama's role is making sure our own crews and any workers we supply arrive ready to follow them — briefed on expectations before deployment, with current tickets, and clear that site procedures take precedence.

Obligations under the Occupational Health and Safety Act 2004 (Vic) and related regulations sit with the principal contractor or site occupier for each project. Nothing on this site changes that.

Quality means it's done once.

A project is only a success if it's done to the standard agreed, on the program agreed, without chasing defects afterwards. Kiama measures itself on that, not on how quickly a job gets started.

If something isn't right, tell us. We'd rather fix it the same week than have you quietly stop calling.
