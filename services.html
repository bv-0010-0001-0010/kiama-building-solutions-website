Services

Built around how you actually live.

Four services that often overlap. A renovation grows into an extension; a new build ends with a deck. Kiama manages the whole scope under one quote.

01 · Renovations

Home renovations & extensions

Whole-house renovations, room additions and second-storey extensions, planned around how you actually live in the home.

Useful when a growing family has outgrown the floorplan, a tired house needs reworking room by room, or an extension would unlock space a block of land can't give you. We manage design input, permits, trades and sequencing so the house stays liveable for as much of the build as possible.

Typical scopeSingle and double-storey extensions, internal reconfigurations, whole-house renovations
TimeframeFrom a few weeks for a single room through to several months for a full extension
PermitsPlanning and building permits arranged as part of the scope where required
QuotingFree, itemised written quote before any work begins

02 · Kitchens & bathrooms

Kitchen & bathroom renovations

Full strip-outs and refits, from layout changes and waterproofing through to the last tile and tapware.

These are the rooms that date a house fastest and matter most day to day. We coordinate plumbing, electrical, waterproofing, tiling, cabinetry and finishes as one sequenced job, not a string of separate trades you have to manage yourself.

Typical scopeFull strip-out and refit, layout changes, waterproofing, tiling, cabinetry, fixtures
TimeframeTypically 2–6 weeks depending on layout changes and material lead times
Trades coordinatedPlumbing, electrical, waterproofing, tiling, carpentry, cabinetry install
ComplianceWaterproofing to AS 3740 and certificates provided on completion

03 · New builds

New home builds

Ground-up residential construction, managed from footings through to keys-in-hand.

Whether it's a knockdown rebuild or a new dwelling on a subdivided block, we run the build as one program — site works, slab, frame, lock-up, fit-out and handover — with one point of contact the whole way through instead of a different name at every stage.

Typical scopeKnockdown rebuilds, new dwellings, secondary dwellings and subdivisions
Stages managedSite works, slab, frame, lock-up, fit-out, handover
TimeframeDepends on scale and council timelines; discussed at quoting stage
ContactOne point of contact across the full build program

04 · Outdoor living

Decks, pergolas & outdoor structures

Decks, pergolas and alfresco structures built to extend how you live outside, not just added on as an afterthought.

Smaller jobs than a full build, but they still deserve proper footings, correct fall and drainage, and finishes that match the house rather than looking bolted on. Often run alongside a kitchen or living-area renovation so the inside and outside come together at the same time.

Typical scopeTimber and composite decking, pergolas, alfresco roofs, outdoor kitchens
TimeframeTypically 1–3 weeks depending on size and council requirements
PermitsChecked against local council requirements before work starts
Often paired withKitchen renovations and rear extensions
